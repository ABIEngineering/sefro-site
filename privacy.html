Privacy policy

Effective date: LAUNCH_DATE_TBD

SEFRO is a notes and journaling app for your computer. The short version: your notes live on your device, and we can’t read them. This page explains, in plain language, the small amount of data the app does handle and why.

Your notes stay on your device

Everything you write in SEFRO — journal entries, notes, folders, images, drawings — is stored locally on your computer. Your notes are never uploaded to us or anyone else. There is no server that holds a copy. If you use the built-in backup/export, the backup file goes wherever you choose to save it, and only there.

What we do collect, and when

1. Your account (optional)

You can use SEFRO without signing in. If you do sign in, we store the email address you sign in with, so you can be recognized across devices. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email — there is no password to store or leak.

2. A few details about you (optional)

After first sign-in, SEFRO may ask for your name, age, and profession, and whether we may email you occasionally about the app. Every field is optional — you can skip them, or fill in only some. If you provide them, they’re stored alongside your account, and we use them to understand who our users are and — only if you opt in — to send you the occasional product update.

3. Feedback you send (only when you press Send)

If you use “Send feedback,” we receive the message you typed, the kind of feedback it is (bug, idea, or question), and a little context so we can act on it: the app version, your platform (Mac or Windows), and which screen you were on. If you’re signed in, your account email and name come along so we can reply; if you’re signed out, you can optionally leave a contact email. Nothing is sent unless you press the button, and we never attach your notes.

4. Crash reports (opt-in only)

The app asks once whether it may send an automatic report if it crashes. If you say no, nothing is ever sent. If you say yes, a crash report includes technical details of the error and the app version, and — if you’re signed in — your account email so we can follow up. Crash reports never include your notes, anything you typed, or a recording of your screen; those are switched off at the code level.

5. Update checks

The app periodically checks GitHub for a newer version. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to GitHub (which hosts our downloads). No personal data is sent with it.

That’s the complete list. SEFRO has no ads, no third-party trackers, and we never sell or rent your data or share it for anyone else’s marketing.

Where this data lives

  • Account, your optional details, and feedback are stored with Supabase (our database provider).
  • Crash reports go to Sentry (a crash-reporting service).
  • Downloads and updates are served by GitHub.

Each provider processes the data only to run its part of the service. They may store it on servers outside your country (for example, in the United States).

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your details — open Settings → Account in the app.
  • Export everything — use the built-in backup/export to get a full copy of your notes at any time.
  • Delete your account — from Settings → Account → Delete account; this removes your account and the details attached to it.
  • Stop the emails — turn off product emails in Settings, or use the unsubscribe link in any email we send.
  • Remove past feedback or crash reports — email us and we’ll delete them.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional legal rights over your data — such as to access it, correct it, or have it erased. We honour those requests; just contact us.

Children

SEFRO isn’t directed at children, and we don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we’ll remove it.
