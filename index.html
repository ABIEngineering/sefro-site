A scratch pad with a spreadsheet’s brain.

One page per day

Open Sefro and today is already there. Yesterday is one keystroke back.

It can do math

The same formulas as Excel and Sheets: =42.80+12.50 =B2+B4 =AVERAGE(A1:A9) — cell references and all, computed right where you write them.

Keyboard-first

Change text, fill a cell with color, set the font, merge and center — all of it straight from the keyboard.

Notes & folders

Not everything is a dated entry. Keep lists, plans, and drafts in folders beside your daily pages.

Your hands stay put

No reaching for the mouse — not even the arrow keys. Everything happens from one resting position: you move fingers, not hands.

Updates itself

Sefro updates quietly in the background. Signing in is optional — the app works fully without it.

Built for people who work in numbers.

Civil engineers, architects, mechanical engineers, carpenters, mathematicians, economists, accountants — Sefro is for anyone who keeps numbers close at hand. It’s a young project: by downloading and testing it, you’re helping shape a new way of scratch-padding.
