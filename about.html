About

Hey — I’m Hussein.

I’m Hussein Abi Nassif, a mechanical engineer. Spreadsheets were always my daily scratchpad — but I could never make them look and feel exactly the way I wanted. So I built my own app.

SEFRO is a tool for getting your thoughts and numbers onto a sheet as fast as possible — offloading your working memory while the page stays beautiful to look at. Same reflexes as Excel and Sheets, prettier paper.

It’s a young project. Every download, every note, every piece of feedback shapes where it goes next.

“ Go spread the sheet. ”
— Hussein
